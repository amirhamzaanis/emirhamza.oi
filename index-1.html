Help

How do I move a piece?

Tap a piece, then tap a highlighted square. Dots show legal moves; rings show captures.

How do special moves work?

Castle by moving your king two squares toward a rook. Capture en passant by moving a pawn diagonally behind one that just advanced two squares. Pawns reaching the last rank become queens.

Computer or two players?

Choose “vs Computer” to play Black against the engine, or “2 Players” to pass one device back and forth.

What do Undo, Flip and Sound do?

Undo takes back your last move, Flip turns the board around, and Sound toggles the move clicks.

How are games won or drawn?

Checkmate wins. Stalemate, when the player to move has no legal move but isn’t in check, is a draw.

Castling, en passant and promotion are all supported.
